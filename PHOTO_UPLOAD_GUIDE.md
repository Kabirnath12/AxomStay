# Photo Upload Guide

Place your own website photos in these folders:

- `public/images/homepage-hero/` - homepage banner photo
- `public/images/assam/` - Assam destination and place photos
- `public/images/meghalaya/` - Meghalaya destination and place photos
- `public/images/arunachal-pradesh/` - Arunachal Pradesh destination and place photos

Recommended formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, or `.webp`.

Recommended sizes:

- Homepage hero: landscape, at least 1800 x 900 pixels
- Destination hero: landscape, at least 1600 x 800 pixels
- Place cards: landscape, at least 1000 x 700 pixels

Use descriptive filenames such as `kaziranga-rhino.jpg`, `shillong-waterfall.jpg`, or `tawang-monastery.jpg`.
